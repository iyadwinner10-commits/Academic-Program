Senior 1 Academic Program

This program is designed for first-year secondary school students who have transitioned from an Arabic to an English-language curriculum. Its primary goal is to empower students to learn independently. Through the lessons offered, students will develop the skills they need to keep up with their curriculum in English.

Math

These lessons build essential Mathematics skills through clear explanations, guided practice, and real-world applications in subjects like Geography and Physics. Students learn to understand, apply, and solve mathematical problems with confidence.

English

These lessons provide an integrated introduction to Academic English, developing students’ grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, and writing skills. Students will learn to understand and use English more effectively in academic contexts, with practice drawn from subjects such as Biology, Geography, and History.
